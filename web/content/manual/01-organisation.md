# Volume 1: Organisation

The UEE 9th Fleet is designed for 1,500 active members in three regional battle groups under Fleet Command. That is 1,429 primary posts, with about 70 people in training at any time. This volume defines every formation, post and grade, the secondary duties, and which posts Task Force Jericho opens first.

## How to read this volume

This is an establishment: a list of posts, not of people. Every post exists on paper now and is filled only once its stage is open.

- **Rank follows the post.** Each post carries a grade or a grade band. Its holder wears that grade's rank in their own service.
- **Grades** use the scale in the roadmap: E1 to E7, OC, and O1 to O10.
- **Opens at** names the roadmap stage at which a post can first be filled.
- **Counts** are primary posts at full strength. They add up to 1,429.
- **A held post** is one the Fleet Commander fills personally until someone is appointed to it.

### Grade bands

Tables show each post's nominal grade. Every post has a band around it, so a member who does well and stays consistent is promoted without changing post.

- **Entry posts** run from E2 to E4.
- **Other posts** run from one grade below the nominal grade to one grade above it.
- **Joining a post.** A member takes a post at their current grade if it is inside the band, or at the bottom of the band if not.
- **Limits.** Enlisted bands stop at E7 and officer bands start at O1. Moving from enlisted to officer always needs the cadet course.
- **Fixed grades.** Commanders of squadrons and larger formations hold the nominal grade only.
- **Authority follows the post.** A Tactical Officer promoted to Lieutenant still answers to the Executive Officer.

Promotion beyond the band still needs a vacancy in a higher post.

## Primary posts and secondary duties

Every member holds one primary post. Staff work that few people want full-time is done as a secondary duty alongside it.

A post is what members call a slot. It is your standing place in a unit, such as rifleman in A Section, and you keep it from one operation to the next. For an event you confirm whether you are attending. You do not choose a post each time. If your post would be empty on the night, the commander puts a stand-in in it.

| Kind | What it is | Rank |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary post | The member's place in a unit. Everyone has exactly one. | Sets the member's grade and rank |
| Secondary duty | A part-time staff job held as well as a primary post. One at most. | None. It goes on the service record. |

The duty rules:

- **Term.** A duty runs for three months and can be renewed.
- **Load.** A duty asks for about four hours a month. The work is spread across a pool instead of loaded onto a few people.
- **Credit.** A duty session counts as an event towards the activity minimum, for up to half of it.
- **Promotion.** One completed term is required for promotion to E5 and to O2.

### Duty pools at full strength

| Duty | Pool | What it covers | Open to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter | 25 | About 100 interview hours a month | E4 and above |
| Instructor | 30 | About 30 training cohorts a month | Holders of the Instructor qualification |
| Staff clerk | 30 | Records, attendance and the ledger for the fleet, battle group and unit staffs | E3 and above |
| Conduct panel | 12 | Cases heard by three members from outside the person's own unit | O3 or E6 and above |
| Planner | 9 | Orders and the opposing force for battle group operations | Officers and senior enlisted |
| Developer | 6 | The platform and the voice app | Anyone with the skill |

About 110 people hold a duty, each doing a little. A senior officer chairs the conduct panel on rotation, so there is no full-time conduct post.

## Names and numbers

Units are numbered off the fleet's number, so any unit's name shows where it belongs.

| Level | Name | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fleet | UEE 9th Fleet | Led by Fleet Command |
| Battle group | 91st (Europe), 92nd (North America), 93rd (Asia-Pacific) | Numbered off the fleet |
| Navy squadron | 901st and 902nd (Europe), 903rd and 904th (North America), 905th (Asia-Pacific) | Numbered in the order they form |
| Ship | UEES Nexus is the first flagship | Frigates take star system names, as lore does. Each squadron names its own. |
| Air wing | A Flight and B Flight within each squadron | Flights are lettered |
| Army | 9th Regiment: 1st Battalion (Europe), 2nd Battalion (North America), 3rd Company Group (Asia-Pacific) | Companies lettered; platoons and sections numbered |
| Marines | 9th Marine Battalion: A Company (Europe), B Company (North America), C Detachment (Asia-Pacific) | Platoons and boarding teams numbered |
| Task force | Task Force Jericho is the launch formation | A named force formed for a task. It can cut across home units. |

Task Force Jericho holds every open post until stage 6. At stage 6 it becomes the 901st Squadron, and at stage 7 the 91st Battle Group forms around it. After that, task force names are used for operations.

The unit numbers avoid the numbers already used in lore: the 2nd and 5th Fleets, the 87th Battle Group, and the 42nd, 128th and 999th Squadrons.

An officer in charge of a vessel is its commanding officer. A non-officer in charge of a vessel is its skipper, never its captain. Captain is used only as a rank.

## Two chains of command

Every member belongs to a home unit and can be assigned to a task force for an operation.

| Chain | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | The unit you belong to, train with and are promoted in | 91st Battle Group, 901st Squadron, UEES Nexus, gunnery |
| Operational | The task organisation for one operation | Task Force Jericho, battle line, UEES Nexus |

- Units stay intact at ship crew, flight, platoon and boarding team level. Mixing happens above that.
- Orders during an operation come down the operational chain only.
- Records, promotion and discipline stay with the home chain.

### Crew templates

Every ship type has a standard crew template, and the table above is the template for an Idris frigate. Templates are fixed once the ship types are chosen.

- **Variants.** A ship type can have variant templates for a role. A variant built for Army support, for example, carries more logistics crew than the standard.
- **Adjustments.** A commanding officer or skipper may ask to change their crew organisation slightly to suit the mission. The next commander up approves it, and Personnel records it.
- **Limits.** An adjustment moves posts between departments. It does not add posts or raise grades. Anything larger is a new variant, approved by the battle group commander, or by the Fleet Commander until stage 7.

## Establishment by grade

At full strength the fleet has 1,429 primary posts. Of these, 169 are officer posts and 113 are senior enlisted posts, so one post in five leads a section, a crew or more. Counts are by nominal grade.

| Service | Primary posts | Officers | E5 to E7 | E4 | Pilots | E1 to E3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Navy and Fleet Command | 783 | 120 | 75 | 93 | 110 | 385 |
| Army | 436 | 37 | 28 | 51 | 0 | 320 |
| Marines | 210 | 12 | 10 | 25 | 0 | 163 |
| Whole fleet | 1,429 | 169 | 113 | 169 | 110 | 868 |

- **Officers by grade:** 1 at O10, 1 at O9, 3 at O8, 5 at O7, 4 at O6, 12 at O5, 21 at O4, 29 at O3, 61 at O2 and 32 at O1.
- **Senior enlisted:** 6 at E7, 27 at E6 and 80 at E5.
- **E4 posts** are team leaders, small-ship skippers, navigators and vehicle commanders.
- **Pilots** are counted apart because their band runs from E3 to E6.
- **In training.** With 1,500 active members, about 70 are recruits, auxiliaries or cadets who hold no post yet.

The officer posts set the size of the cadet school. If 5% of officers lapse each month, the school must commission about eight a month. That means about 17 cadets on the eight-week course at any time.

## Launch order of battle

Task Force Jericho opens posts stage by stage, starting with seven on a training ship and reaching UEES Nexus at stage 2.

| Stage | Active strength | Posts that open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Cadre | 1 to 7 | Fleet Commander, who also commands the training ship. Helm, one engineer and up to four gunners. |
| 2 Ship's company | 8 to 15 | The UEES Nexus watch bill of eight. Then the Executive Officer, Chief Engineer, Gunnery Chief, Signaller, Medic and more gunners. The first Instructors. |
| 3 Ship and air wing | 16 to 30 | The full flagship crew of 25. The first section of A Flight, four pilots. One gunship escort. Staff section heads. |
| 4 Task group | 31 to 55 | A second escort. The lift flight. The Army's first section, then its first platoon. The planners. |
| 5 Task force | 56 to 90 | The rest of A Flight. The support flight. The Marine ship security detachment. A corvette as the second major crew. |

- More posts are open at each stage than there are members, so people have a choice and vacancies are visible.
- Until the first cadet course graduates, officer posts are filled by acting appointments.
- Instructing and recruiting are secondary duties from the start, held alongside a crew post.
